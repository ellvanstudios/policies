Privacy Policy for Floating Town: Village Builder

Last updated: November 3, 2026

This policy explains how Ellvan Studios and the services used by Floating Town: Village Builder (the “Game”) process information. Features and providers depend on the platform and app version: Android versions may include purchases, rewarded advertising, gameplay analytics and advertising attribution. Browser previews may not include these services.

1. Local villages and settings

Villages, building layouts, resources, tutorial progress, save names and settings are stored on your device. We do not include save names or villager names in our gameplay analytics. Do not enter sensitive personal information in names. Android or browser backup may retain local data according to your account and device settings.

You can delete saved islands in the Game or remove local data through Android app-storage settings or your browser’s site-data controls. Uninstalling or clearing local data does not delete purchase records or the online currency balance associated with your game account.

2. Google Play Games, purchases and PlayFab

The Android Game uses Google Play Games Services for supported sign-in and account recovery. Microsoft PlayFab processes game-account identifiers and authentication tokens, currency balances, inventory, entitlements and purchase/redemption history so we can deliver Sky Gems and other purchases, validate transactions, prevent duplicate grants and restore the unspent balance associated with the same account. Browser versions may use a randomly generated installation identifier.

Google Play processes payments. Purchase product identifiers and purchase tokens are sent to PlayFab for validation. Ellvan Studios does not receive your Google password or full payment-card details. Restoring an online balance requires access to the same linked account; local islands are separate from that balance.

Read the Google Privacy Policy and Microsoft Privacy Statement.

3. Gameplay analytics

Android releases use Google Firebase Analytics to understand screens visited, tutorial progress and gameplay actions, such as building and objective activity. This can include app-instance identifiers, device and app information and session/interaction measurements. The Game’s “Share gameplay analytics” setting controls this Firebase gameplay collection. Turning it off stops new gameplay events and requests a reset of analytics data on the device; it does not delete records already processed by the service.

The Game configures Firebase advertising storage, advertising user data and advertising personalisation purposes as denied. This Firebase setting is separate from the advertising and attribution services described below. See Firebase privacy information.

4. Rewarded ads and advertising attribution

The Game offers optional rewarded ads, for example to receive a gem gift or help finish construction. Depending on the build, ads are supplied through Google AdMob or Appodeal and its mediated advertising partners. Appodeal builds also include AppsFlyer and Meta Analytics adapters for install attribution and campaign measurement when configured. These services help deliver ads, measure impressions and interactions, identify which campaign led to an installation, detect fraud and understand retention and session activity.

Advertising and attribution providers may process your IP address, approximate location inferred from it, device and advertising identifiers where available and permitted, app/OS version, device characteristics, install/referrer and campaign information, session activity, ad requests, views, clicks and diagnostic information. The Game does not request GPS/location permission for advertising. The actual providers and permitted processing depend on the build, region, audience restrictions and your choices.

Where required, a consent message provides choices before advertising processing. Use Settings → Ad privacy choices to revisit the available consent or opt-out options. Refusing consent does not block village building, but some advertising or reward opportunities may be unavailable. Android also offers advertising-ID reset/deletion controls. The Firebase gameplay toggle does not disable Appodeal, AppsFlyer or Meta attribution.

The Android integration currently requests child-directed ad treatment. This is a restriction sent to the advertising SDK, not a claim that no device/network information is processed. We do not knowingly request personalised advertising for children. Parents or guardians can contact us about children’s data.

5. Support and other disclosures

If you contact us, we receive the information you send, such as your email address, message, screenshots and an account or transaction identifier needed to investigate a problem. Do not send passwords, authentication tokens or full payment details. Opening external links connects you to the relevant website under its own policies.

We use service providers for account functionality, payment fulfilment, analytics, advertising and measurement as described above. Information may also be disclosed where required by law, to protect players and our services, or in connection with a business transfer subject to applicable safeguards. Service providers may process information in countries other than yours under their published safeguards.

6. Retention, security and deletion requests

Local saves remain until deleted or removed by your device. Online account, inventory and purchase records are retained as needed to provide recovery, fulfil purchases, address fraud or disputes and meet legal obligations; providers apply their own retention rules. Analytics and advertising records follow the relevant provider’s retention settings and policies. We do not promise that clearing the app erases records held by those services.

To request access, correction or deletion of data associated with the Game, email ellvanstudios@gmail.com with “Floating Town privacy request” and enough non-secret information to identify the account. We may need to verify your request. We will explain any records we must retain and any consequences for account recovery or purchases. You may also exercise applicable rights directly with Google, Microsoft or an advertising provider.

We use platform access controls and service-provider security features, but no storage or transmission method is completely secure. Keep your device and linked accounts protected.

7. Changes and contact

We update this page when the Game’s data practices change. The date above identifies the current version. For privacy questions contact Ellvan Studios at ellvanstudios@gmail.com.

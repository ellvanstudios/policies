Privacy Policy — Voxel Builder

Effective 17 July 2026 · Last updated 17 July 2026

Voxel Builder is a 3D voxel-building app published by Ellvan Studios (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what the app does — and does not do — with your information. In short: the app does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information about you.

Information we collect

None. Voxel Builder has no user accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics, advertising, or tracking software. We do not operate a server that receives your data, and the app does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, camera, microphone, or device identifiers.

Data stored on your device

Everything you create and change stays on your device, in the app's local storage. This includes:

  • Your saved builds and in-progress worlds
  • App settings and preferences
  • In-app currency balances and which content packs or templates you own
  • A local flag recording whether you have purchased the “Unlock Everything” upgrade

This information never leaves your device by our doing. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes it.

Device backups. If you have Android's backup feature enabled, the operating system may copy the app's local data to your own Google Drive account as part of your device backup. This is a feature of your device and Google account, under your control — the data goes to your Google account, not to us. You can manage or disable it in your device's backup settings.

Purchases

Voxel Builder offers a one-time in-app purchase (“Unlock Everything”). Payments are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see or receive your payment card details. Google provides us only with aggregate, anonymised order and payout information through the Play Console. Google's handling of your purchase is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

Permissions the app requests

  • Internet — required by the app's runtime and by Google Play's billing system to verify a purchase. The app does not use it to send us personal data.
  • Billing — allows the in-app purchase to be processed through Google Play.

Children

Voxel Builder is suitable for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the page and revise the “Last updated” date above. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the revised policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email us at ellvanstudios@gmail.com.

Purchases & refunds

Voxel Builder's in-app purchases are handled by Google Play. For refund requests, please use Google Play's refund process. If you purchased “Unlock Everything” but it isn't showing as active, try the Restore purchase option in the app, then contact us if it still doesn't appear.
